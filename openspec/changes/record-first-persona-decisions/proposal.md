# Proposal

## Why

After reviewing and merging the desk study in PR #4, the maintainer requested the
next product decisions and supplied answers on corpus priorities and memory.
Future chats need a durable record separating confirmed intent from unresolved
offline/data-flow, automatic-memory and implementation choices.

## What Changes

- Record TXT/born-digital PDF, French/English and an approximately 1,000-page
  planning corpus; preserve the confirmed Windows household-PC target.
- Record automatic, inspectable and deletable persona memories with immediate
  use, preserved conversational origin and sharing across the same persona's
  interlocutors, without silently requiring manual approval.
- Compare local, owned-remote and hosted inference options; identify outstanding
  data-flow approvals and an unexecuted evaluation proposal.
- Update the decision register, evaluation context, research status, roadmap,
  README, OpenSpec context and CHANGELOG.
- Document native issue/branch/PR linkage, closure keywords and the distinction
  between branch deletion settings and manual merging.
- Deliver through [issue #5](https://github.com/Eneru/soulkiller/issues/5)
  and a linked draft PR.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None implemented. This change records product intent and questions as documentation.

### Modified Capabilities

None implemented. Use `skip_specs: true`: no application contract or architecture
is adopted. The recorded intent will inform a separate reviewed change with
behavior requirements and scenarios before code.

## Impact

Markdown and OpenSpec YAML only. No devcontainer/dependency/service changes,
model downloads, benchmarks, provider calls, data transfers or repository-setting
changes. The corpus size is not a tested capacity, and comparison is not egress
authorization. LICENSE remains unchanged.
