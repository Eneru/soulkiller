# Proposal

## Why

The maintainer approved the feasibility-study topic in PR #2 and requested the
next step. Soulkiller needs evidence-backed options for authorized local
multimodal extraction and digital-persona conversations before implementing
product behavior or adopting a stack.

## What Changes

- Deliver a desk-research report comparing extraction, retrieval, prompting,
  fine-tuning, language choices, deployment and OmniRoute.
- Preserve primary-source references and distinguish documented capabilities,
  engineering hypotheses, maintainer answers and unmeasured performance.
- Provide an evaluation proposal using synthetic fixtures and a decision register
  with tradeoffs, recommendations, open questions and follow-up issue drafts.
- Update README, roadmap, the approved brief and CHANGELOG to describe the study
  and its tracking issue: [#3](https://github.com/Eneru/soulkiller/issues/3).
- Record the confirmed priority: Windows source machines on ordinary household
  PCs. CPU-only operation is a working assumption; exact hardware is open.

The approved topic permits comparison and recommendations, not stack adoption,
product implementation, new dependency installations or resource-dependent
experiments. No personal corpus, provider credentials or paid calls are needed.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None. This change delivers research documentation, not application behavior.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Use `skip_specs: true` for this documentation-only change. Proposed product
behavior remains subject to maintainer decisions and a separate OpenSpec change.
The foundation's documentation and English-language requirements remain intact.

## Impact

Documentation and OpenSpec research-tracking artifacts only. No devcontainer,
API, dependency, service, repository setting or workflow change. Submit results
in a draft PR; the maintainer reviews recommendations before implementation.
